# 5. 1.1.0 -> 1.2.0

*Sect. 5 of the upgrade guide, `../UPGRADING.md`: read its opening first, for `SRC`, `REPO`, `SCRATCH` and `CLI_DIR`. A bare "sect. N" here is that guide's sect. N, and its index names the file. `ADOPTING.md` is `../ADOPTING.md`, an index too.*

1.2.0 adds one optional `config.json` key, `icon_color`, the background of the icons. A new key is a configuration change, so this is a minor release (user rule, 2026-09-30: "Minor only updates when a configuration or json structure change happens"). The schema, `tags.json` and your tickets are untouched. The upgrade is the general procedure of sect. 3: a plain copy of the tool folder except `icons/`, the new skill, and the key if you want a colour. Read `CHANGELOG.md` [1.2.0] first. A copy on 1.0.0 does sect. 4 first.

## What behaves differently

- **The icon colour (B31).** `make_icons.py` paints the background from `icon_color`, by default `#007acc`, Visual Studio blue. Without the key, your icons come out byte-identical to 1.1.0's. `--color "#rrggbb"` overrides the key for one bake. Quote it: unquoted, the `#` starts a comment in bash and in PowerShell. `make_icons.py --text XY` now reads `config.json` when it finds one, for the colour.
- **A bad `icon_color` stops whatever reads `config.json`.** The CLI, the viewer and `make_icons.py` print `ERROR .claude/tickets/config.json: icon_color must be a colour written #rrggbb (e.g. #007acc), not "..."` and exit 1. `serve.py --stop` still works (B25). A bad `--color` prints `ERROR --color must be a colour written #rrggbb (e.g. #007acc), not '...'`.
- **A value ending in a line break is rejected (B34).** Every anchored check now tests the whole value: `id_prefix`, tag names and colours, `icon_color`, `server_version`, ids and links, and the schema's patterns. Before, `"XY-"` plus a newline passed as an id prefix. The usual one-line `ERROR` names the value, with the line break written as `\n`. Every adopter's store known at the time was checked and still validates.
- **`identify` reads `tool_dir` (B32),** before the two default folders (sect. 1). A copy whose tool is elsewhere, such as in `project/tools/tickets`, is found without `--cli-dir` once `tool_dir` names it.
- **The skill (B29, B30).** Its description names more of the bare requests it answers, such as "new ticket", "log a finding", "list tickets" and "set a status". Its text names the rules doc's sections instead of citing their numbers, and the Settings "Rules doc" row maps each name to your number.

## Steps

1. **File the upgrade and set it `in_progress`** (sect. 3 step 1).
   **Check:** `list --active` shows it.
2. **Export 1.2.0** (sect. 3 step 2): `release.py export 1.2.0 <SCRATCH>/ts-1.2.0`.
   **Check:** `PAYLOAD/../VERSION` reads `1.2.0`.
3. **Back up and stop the viewer** (sect. 3 steps 3 and 4).
   **Check:** `--stop` reports the viewer stopped, or nothing recorded.
4. **Replace the tool folder, except `icons/`** (sect. 3 step 5): copy everything in `PAYLOAD/src/tools/tickets/` except `icons/` over `<CLI_DIR>/`, or just the three files 1.2.0 changed: `make_icons.py`, `serve.py` and `tickets.py`. 1.2.0 removes no file, and nothing is edited after the copy.
   **Check:** `python <CLI_DIR>/tickets.py validate` passes on your existing tickets.
5. **Choose the icon colour (optional).** Leave `icon_color` out for the default blue. To keep the colour your icons had, add it to `config.json`, 2-space indented, UTF-8 with LF, such as `"icon_color": "#68217a"`.

   To find your 1.0.0 colour, look at the `background = (...)` line of your 1.0.0 `make_icons.py`, in your backup or your history. `(0x00, 0x7a, 0xcc)` is `#007acc`, the default, which needs no key; the release's own 1.0.0 icons were that blue. A copy adopted at 1.1.0 has the default too. Every 1.1.0 re-bake was blue, whatever it had before (sect. 4 step 7). If you kept your 1.0.0 icons at 1.1.0, setting the key makes the re-bake in step 6 keep their colour, with 1.1.0's larger letters.
   **Check:** `python <CLI_DIR>/tickets.py validate` still passes. A bad value stops it with the `icon_color` `ERROR` above.
6. **Re-bake the icons** (sect. 3 step 6): `python <CLI_DIR>/make_icons.py`. Only needed if you set `icon_color` in step 5: step 4 kept your old icons, and without the key a re-bake is byte-identical to 1.1.0's.
   **Check:** three `wrote icons/... (..., text <your icon text>, colour <your colour>)` lines.
7. **The schema and the rules doc.** The schema is unchanged. Your rules doc keeps your project text and your section numbers. Take from `PAYLOAD/dot-claude/docs/tickets.md`:
   - sect. 1's `config.json` keys table: the new `icon_color` row, and the `tool_dir` row, which `identify` reads now too;
   - sect. 1's `make_icons.py` row and sect. 4's icon line: the icons are baked on `icon_color`.

   **Check:** `grep -n "icon_color" .claude/docs/tickets.md` finds the key's row.
8. **The skill.** Take `PAYLOAD/dot-claude/skills/tickets/SKILL.md` whole, put your Settings rows back, and set the version row to `1.2.0`.
   - **The Rules doc row** now maps the sections the skill names to your numbers: `the severity scale sect. N, the rules section sect. N, the peer exchange section sect. N, the ticket-system section sect. N`. The shipped row gives the template's numbers: 2, 5, 5a and 6. **Keep your own numbers, and map them in the row.** Many copies differ from the template: a peer exchange split over two sections (map both, such as `5a and 6`), or a ticket-system section numbered `6a` or `5b`. Check yours against your doc.
   - Have a separate read-only agent review it, and install it only after a pass.

   **Check:** the review passed, and each number in the Rules doc row leads to the section it names in your rules doc.
9. **Line endings (recommended, B33).** Add the `.gitattributes` lines from `ADOPTING.md` step 9, with your `CLI_DIR` filled in: `<CLI_DIR>/** text eol=lf`, then `<CLI_DIR>/icons/*.png binary`, `.claude/skills/tickets/** text eol=lf`, `.claude/hooks/tickets/** text eol=lf`, and the `.claude/tickets/*.json text eol=lf` line you may have already. Why: with `core.autocrlf=true`, the Git for Windows default, a branch switch checks these files out again with CRLF, and `identify` still matches them, but the bytes differ from the release. A file already checked out with CRLF keeps it until git writes it again; the copies in steps 4 and 8 are the release's LF files.
   **Check:** `git check-attr text eol -- <CLI_DIR>/tickets.py` prints `text: set` and `eol: lf`, and `git check-attr text -- <CLI_DIR>/icons/icon-192.png` prints `text: unset`.
10. **The hooks,** if you installed them: sect. 6, "Upgrading installed hooks".
11. **Verify** (sect. 3 step 10):
    - `python <CLI_DIR>/tickets.py validate` passes;
    - start the viewer again, and `/api/health` answers with your marker;
    - the page shows your tickets under the same title, with your icon in your colour;
    - `identify`: the `release 1.2.0` block shows every product file matching, the icons `(yours: adapted per project, not compared)`, and the skill `settings-only`.
12. **Commit, record, tell.** The version row says `1.2.0` (step 8). Commit the way your project does. Tell the maintainer you run 1.2.0, and close the upgrade ticket on that evidence.
